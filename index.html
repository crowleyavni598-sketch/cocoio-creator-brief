Preparation guide

Hot & cold use.

The supplied instruction graphic shows the following preparation steps. Follow the instructions included with the exact product you receive; do not assume every style has identical heating or cooling directions.

Cold preparation

Freeze for 2–3 hours with the included bag. If it feels too cold, let it sit before use. The graphic advises placing a towel between the product and skin.

Hot preparation

The graphic says to remove the plastic bag first, microwave for 30 seconds, then add 10-second increments as needed. Confirm that this method applies to the exact item and follow its packaging directions.

Safety: Never apply a product that feels uncomfortably hot or cold directly to skin. Use the supplied instructions for your specific item and stop use if discomfort occurs.
